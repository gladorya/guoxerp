Do I need a TikTok account to use it?

Yes. The application publishes to TikTok on behalf of an account you own, authorized through TikTok's official Login Kit. It cannot publish to an account you do not control, and it never asks for your TikTok password.

Does it post automatically without asking me?

No. Every upload is one you selected, with a caption you wrote. In draft mode nothing goes public at all until you open the TikTok app and tap Post yourself.

Can it get me more followers?

The application handles the publishing side: reliable uploads, a steady schedule, clean captions. Growth comes from your content. The application never buys followers and never automates engagement, because that breaks TikTok's rules and gets accounts banned.

Why did my upload land as a draft instead of my profile?

TikTok requires applications that are still in review to publish as drafts: the video lands in your TikTok inbox and you tap Post there. Once the application is approved for direct posting, drafts become optional and direct posting works normally.

Why was my upload rejected as spam?

TikTok limits how fast accounts can publish, especially newer ones and especially when several videos are waiting unpublished. Wait a while, publish what is already in your inbox, and space your uploads. The application detects this and backs off automatically instead of pushing harder, which is what makes such blocks last.

Where is my data stored?

On your own computer, in the application's own folder. There is no server on our side that holds your tokens or your videos. See the privacy policy for the complete picture.

Can it publish to two accounts at once?

Yes, if you have connected and authorized both. Each account is handled separately with its own schedule, its own stats and its own posting history, so a burst on one never affects the other.

What video formats are supported?

MP4 and MOV, vertical or horizontal, at least a few seconds long. Vertical videos fill the screen; horizontal ones are framed on a background. TikTok decides the final presentation.

Can I delete a video after publishing it?

Yes. Open My videos, enter the video id of the post, and confirm. The application asks TikTok to remove it and reports the result.

Does it work with Instagram or YouTube?

TikTok is the platform the application integrates with today. It was built so other platforms can be added later, but TikTok is what it does now.

Is my TikTok password stored anywhere?

No. Authorization happens through TikTok's own window and the application never sees your password. It only receives a publishing token, which is stored locally on your machine.

How do I stop using it?

Close the application and remove its authorization in your TikTok settings, under Security and permissions, then Manage app authorizations. The application keeps nothing after that.

Who made it?

An independent developer who publishes clips and wanted the upload part to be reliable. The support page lists the ways to reach us.
