Version 1.4.0 · November 2026

  • English titles and captions written from the clip's own audio: the application transcribes the speech, translates it, and turns it into a clean title that describes the moment.
  • Automatic text rules: published captions never contain profanity, shouting, filler phrases or punctuation noise, enforced by the application itself.
  • Scheduling that respects your audience: posts go out when your viewers are awake rather than when the machine happens to be on.
  • Platform blocks are respected: when TikTok asks the application to slow down, it waits instead of retrying, and it tells you how long it will wait.
  • Upload audio and video now always end together.

Version 1.3.0 · November 2026

  • Performance dashboard: follower counts and per video statistics read live from TikTok.
  • Post management: published videos can be removed from inside the application by video id.
  • Multiple accounts with per account schedules, statistics and posting history.
  • Upload progress is shown in real time during the transfer.

Version 1.2.0 · November 2026

  • Draft mode: send videos to your TikTok inbox for a final review inside TikTok before they go public.
  • Captions burned into the video from the clip's own speech, with automatic profanity filtering and clean line timing.
  • Caption timing follows the speech in the video, including translated speech.

Version 1.1.0 · November 2026

  • Chunked uploads for larger videos, with automatic retries when TikTok asks for them.
  • Account health display: the dashboard shows which accounts are connected, which need attention, and why.
  • Automatic token refresh, so a connected account stays connected.

Version 1.0.0 · November 2026

  • First release: TikTok authorization through Login Kit, video upload through the Content Posting API, direct post and draft modes, and the local dashboard.
