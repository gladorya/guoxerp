User guide

From connecting your account to managing published posts, step by step.

Connect your account

Click Connect TikTok account on the dashboard. TikTok's authorization window opens in your browser. Sign in with the account you want to publish to, review the requested permissions, and click Allow.

The application receives a publishing token and shows your account as connected, with your username and profile picture. To add another account, repeat the same flow and switch between accounts from the selector at the top of the dashboard.

If authorization fails: make sure you are signed in to the correct TikTok account in that browser, and that you clicked Allow on the permission screen rather than closing the window.

Upload a video

Drag a video file onto the upload area, or click it to browse. MP4 and MOV files are supported, vertical or horizontal. The application validates the file against TikTok's requirements before the upload starts, and shows progress while it transfers.

When the upload completes you get the video id. Keep it: it is how you identify the post later if you want to manage it.

Choose your publishing mode

Direct post publishes the video to your profile immediately with the caption you wrote.

Draft sends the video to your TikTok inbox instead. You review it in the TikTok app, adjust the cover or the caption there if you want, and tap Post. Drafts are the safe choice while you are learning what the application does.

Write a caption that works

The caption is yours. A good one says what happens in the moment, credits the original creator if the clip is not yours to begin with, and asks one question that invites replies. Keep the first line strong: that is what most viewers read before deciding to watch.

The application filters profanity from captions automatically, because TikTok age restricts videos with profane titles and captions.

Track your performance

The dashboard reads your account statistics live from TikTok: follower count, video count, and the views and likes on each published video. Everything updates when the dashboard refreshes, so you are always looking at current numbers rather than yesterday's.

Manage published posts

Open My videos to see what is published. Each entry shows the video id, the view count and the like count. To remove a post, enter its video id in the Delete post field and confirm. The application asks TikTok to take it down and reports the result.

Good captions, briefly

  • Say what happens in the moment: "The police lose the player after a garage swap" beats "insane moment".
  • Keep the first line under about 70 characters. Most viewers read only that before deciding.
  • Credit the original creator in the caption. The application adds the credit line automatically.
  • One emoji is plenty. Zero is also fine.
  • No hashtags in the first line: they push the part people read further down.

Troubleshooting

  • Authorization fails. Check that the browser is signed in to the TikTok account you meant to connect, and that you clicked Allow rather than closing the window.
  • An upload is rejected as spam. TikTok limits how fast accounts can publish, especially newer ones. Wait a while, publish the drafts already waiting in your TikTok inbox first, and space your uploads. The application backs off automatically when this happens.
  • A draft never appears in your inbox. Drafts can take a few minutes to process on TikTok's side. If it still does not appear after ten minutes, remove it in the application and upload again.
  • The dashboard shows an account as not connected. The token expired or was revoked. Connect the account again through the authorization flow.
