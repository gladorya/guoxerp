Why it exists

Guoxerp Poster started as an internal tool. Publishing clips every day by hand was slow, error prone and hard to keep consistent across accounts. The upload part of that work is mechanical, so it was automated: connect the account once, then publish what you choose with the caption you wrote, on a schedule that makes sense.

The tool grew into something worth sharing, and the rules it was built under became the rules on this site: publish only what the account owner selected, keep the data on the user's machine, credit creators, honor takedown requests, and never automate engagement.

What it will do

Reliable publishing through TikTok's official APIs, better scheduling, better dashboards, and support for the platforms creators actually use. Everything that makes the publishing side of a clip channel less work.

What it will never do

No follower buying, no engagement automation, no scraping other people's content, no posting to accounts without their own authorization, and no dark patterns to trick users into anything. The application is a tool for the account owner, full stop.

The developer

An independent developer who runs clip channels and builds tools for them. Contact options are on the support page.
