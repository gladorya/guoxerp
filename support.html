How to get help, report a problem, or suggest an improvement.

Start here

Most questions are answered in the FAQ, and the user guide walks through the whole flow from connecting an account to publishing a video. If your question is about a rejected upload, the FAQ covers the most common cause: TikTok limiting how fast accounts can publish.

Contact

Open an issue on the project's GitHub page, or reach the developer through the application's support option. Reports are answered in the order they arrive, usually within two business days.

What to include

  • The application version (from the changelog).
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • The exact error text if the application showed one.
  • Do not send your TikTok token or password. The application never needs them for support, and nobody should ever ask you for them.

Publishing mistakes

If a post went out with the wrong caption, or a video published that should not have, include the video id shown in the dashboard and the time it was published. Publishing mistakes are treated as the highest priority.

Feature requests

Requests that help creators publish their own content more reliably are welcome. Requests that automate engagement, buy followers, or publish other people's videos are not, and will not be built.
