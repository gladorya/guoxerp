Guoxerp Poster - Terms of Service

Effective Date: October 11, 2026

1. What This App Does

Guoxerp Poster is a utility that schedules and publishes video content to TikTok on behalf of authenticated users. It uses TikTok's Login Kit for authentication and Content Posting API for video posting.

2. User Responsibilities

You are responsible for the content you post. You must comply with TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.

3. Data We Store

We store only the access token required for posting. We do not store video content, browsing history, or personal data beyond your display name.

4. Access Revocation

You may revoke Guoxerp's access to your TikTok account at any time through TikTok's privacy settings.

5. Contact

Questions? Contact us through our app.
