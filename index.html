Features

Everything a clip channel needs

Built for creators who publish clips and highlights regularly, and want the upload side handled properly.

Official Login Kit authorization

You sign in through TikTok's own authorization window. The app never sees your password and can only act on the account you approved.

Direct post and drafts

Publish straight to your profile, or land the video in your TikTok inbox as a draft you review and post yourself. Both run through the Content Posting API.

Desktop dashboard

Account stats, upload progress and posting history in one window on your own computer. Your tokens and settings never leave your machine.

Sensible scheduling

Uploads keep a minimum gap between posts, so your profile stays active all day instead of posting in bursts and going quiet.

Live performance stats

Follower count, video count, views and likes on your published posts, read live from TikTok every time the dashboard refreshes.

Post management

See your published videos with their ids and stats, and remove any post from inside the app when it turns out to be wrong for your page.

Captions that read well

Clip speech is transcribed and translated, then turned into clean English captions burned into the video. Profanity is filtered automatically.

Private by design

Tokens and settings stay on your machine. No account on our side holds your data, and the application talks only to TikTok's own servers.

How it works

Four steps, start to finish

Connect your account

Click Connect and TikTok's authorization window opens. Sign in as yourself, approve the publishing permission, and the dashboard shows your account as connected. The app receives a publishing token, never your password.

Pick your video

Drag any video onto the upload area. MP4 and MOV both work, vertical or horizontal. The app validates the file against TikTok's requirements before anything is sent.

Write your caption

Say what happens in the moment, credit the original creator, add your own touch. You see the full caption before anything is sent.

Publish or review

Send it straight to your profile, or send it to your TikTok inbox as a draft and post it from there. Either way the dashboard records it with a link.

Good to know

The rules the app follows

Guoxerp Poster publishes only what you select, only to accounts you authorized, and only through TikTok's official APIs. It does not buy followers, does not automate likes or comments, does not touch accounts you did not connect, and does not hold your data on any server. When TikTok asks the app to slow down, it backs off and waits instead of pushing harder. These rules are part of the application, not settings you can accidentally change.
