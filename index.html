Guoxerp Poster

A desktop tool that helps content creators publish and schedule their own videos to TikTok using official account authorization.

What it does

Guoxerp Poster connects to a creator's own TikTok account through TikTok's official OAuth flow and posts videos the creator has made, when the creator tells it to. It is built for people who manage their own content across devices and want their uploads queued and published reliably — including directly to the creator's TikTok inbox as drafts the creator reviews and posts.

Features

  • Official TikTok Login Kit authorization — you always sign in as yourself
  • Upload videos you created, with your own captions and hashtags
  • Draft mode: uploads land in your TikTok inbox for you to review and post
  • Per-account posting schedules so uploads never spam your audience
  • Runs locally on your own computer — your credentials never leave your device

How it works

  1. Authorize your TikTok account through TikTok's official authorization window
  2. Pick a video you made and write your own caption
  3. Choose direct post or draft, and Guoxerp Poster handles the upload

What it is not

Guoxerp Poster does not buy followers, does not automate engagement (likes, comments, follows), and does not post content on behalf of anyone other than the authorized account owner. It follows TikTok's Community Guidelines and API terms, and it posts nothing you did not choose to publish.
