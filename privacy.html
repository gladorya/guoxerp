Guoxerp Poster - Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 11, 2026

1. What We Collect

We collect your TikTok display name and open ID when you authenticate. We store the access token needed to post videos.

2. What We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect your password, email, phone number, browsing history, video content, contacts, or location data.

3. How We Use Data

Your display name is used to identify you in the app. The access token is used exclusively to post videos you explicitly request.

4. Data Storage

All data is stored locally on the user's own machine. No data is transmitted to third parties.

5. Your Rights

You may revoke Guoxerp's access to your TikTok account at any time through TikTok's settings. Revoking access deletes the stored token.

6. Contact

Questions? Contact us through our app.
