Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026. Guoxerp Poster ("the app") is a desktop tool that publishes videos to TikTok on behalf of the authorized account owner. This policy explains exactly what data the app touches and what it does not.

1. What data the app accesses

  • TikTok authorization tokens — obtained through TikTok's official Login Kit when you authorize your own account. These are required to upload on your behalf.
  • Video files and captions — the files you select for upload and the text you write for them.
  • Basic profile information — your TikTok display name and open identifier, used to show which account is connected.

2. Where your data lives

Everything is stored locally on your own computer. The app has no central server that collects your data. Tokens never leave your device except in the direct API calls to TikTok's own endpoints needed to perform the upload you requested.

3. What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.
  • We do not read your TikTok messages, contacts, or browsing activity.
  • We do not post, like, comment, or follow without your explicit action.
  • We do not use your content for advertising or model training.

4. Content ownership

You keep every right to the videos you publish through the app. Guoxerp Poster claims no license over your content beyond the technical upload you request.

5. Data deletion

Deleting the app's data folder removes all stored tokens locally. To revoke the app's access to your TikTok account entirely, open TikTok → Settings → Security and permissions → Manage app authorizations, and remove Guoxerp Poster. You can also contact us (see footer of the homepage) and we will confirm that no server-side copy of your data exists — because there is none.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date at the top will change with it. Material changes will be announced in the app before they take effect.
