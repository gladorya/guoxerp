Privacy Policy

What the application touches, where it is stored, and what never leaves your machine. Last updated: November 27, 2026.

The short version

Guoxerp Poster runs on your computer. Your tokens, videos, captions and posting history stay on your machine, in the application's own folder. There is no account on our side, no cloud storage, no analytics and no advertising. The only external service the application ever talks to is TikTok, through its official APIs, to do exactly what you asked it to do: publish your videos and read your account's public statistics.

What the application accesses

  • A publishing token issued by TikTok when you connect an account through Login Kit. This is what lets the application publish on that account. It is stored locally and refreshed automatically before it expires.
  • Your account's public identity as TikTok reports it once you authorize: username, display name and profile picture. Only for accounts you connect yourself.
  • The video files you choose to upload, plus the caption and title you write or approve for each one.
  • Statistics for videos published through the application: follower count, video count, views and likes, read from TikTok when the dashboard refreshes.
  • Local settings: schedules, account list, application preferences.

What the application never touches

Your TikTok password (authorization happens in TikTok's own window, so the application never sees it), your private messages, your other social accounts, files you did not select, contacts, browsing history, or any data from accounts you did not connect. There is no telemetry: the application does not phone home about anything, ever.

What is sent to TikTok

Only what publishing requires: the video file, its caption text, and the API requests that upload it and read back your account's statistics. Once data reaches TikTok, TikTok's own privacy policy governs how it is handled. No other third party receives anything from the application, because there is nothing else connected.

Where data lives and how to delete it

Everything the application stores lives in its own folder on your computer. To erase all of it: disconnect your accounts inside the application, delete the application folder, and revoke the authorizations in your TikTok settings under Security and permissions, then Manage app authorizations. Revoking stops the token from working immediately, even before the local data is removed.

Removing a published video removes it from TikTok as well, which you can do from inside the application by video id.

Children

The application is not directed at children under 13 and is not meant for them. Accounts connected through the application must already meet TikTok's own age requirements, since authorization happens inside TikTok's own system.

Changes to this policy

If the application ever starts touching new categories of data, this page changes first and the change is noted in the changelog of the version that introduces it. What is written here describes what the current version actually does, not an aspiration.

Contact

Questions about this policy go through the support page or the project's GitHub page. Takedown requests for a specific published video should include the video id and are treated as the highest priority.
