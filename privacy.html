Privacy Policy

Guoxerp Poster

Last Updated: October 11, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Guoxerp Poster ("the Service") handles user data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and minimizing data collection.

2. Data We Collect

When you authenticate via TikTok Login Kit, we receive:

  • Display Name: Used to identify you within the app
  • Open ID: A TikTok-generated identifier
  • Access Token: Required to post videos on your behalf

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Passwords or login credentials
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Location data
  • Browsing history
  • Contacts or address books
  • Device information
  • Video content beyond posting
  • Biometric data
  • Payment information

4. How We Use Data

Your display name is shown in the app interface. Your access token is used exclusively to publish videos you explicitly request. No other use is made of any collected data.

5. Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device. We do not use cloud storage, third-party servers, or databases. Your data never leaves your computer except when communicating directly with TikTok's API.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share, or transfer your data to any third party. The only external communication is with TikTok's official API for the purpose of posting your content.

7. Data Retention

Access tokens are retained until you revoke access or uninstall the app. Display name and Open ID are retained for the same duration. No data is retained after account disconnection.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: View what data is stored (available in the app)
  • Deletion: Remove all stored data (revoke TikTok access or uninstall)
  • Portability: Export your data (not applicable — minimal data)
  • Revocation: Disconnect Guoxerp Poster from TikTok at any time

9. Data Security

Access tokens are stored encrypted on your local device. We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics services.

10. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Continued use constitutes acceptance of updates.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us through the Guoxerp Poster application.
